# Mission check-in

A drifted mission poisons every plan made from it: the frontier ranks
toward an outcome the learner no longer wants. Re-run the mission interview
as a check-in. The outcome may be a re-confirmed mission, a revised
statement with new exclusions or priority, or a retired mission; each is
recorded as a new Mission Observation, never an edit.

Reads: `mission(scope?)`, `preferences()`.

@../snippets/mission-interview.md

If the check-in reveals a genuinely new goal with no coverage, suggest
charting. If the mission stands but its slice is exhausted, extend the
slice through charting's inventory step rather than here.
