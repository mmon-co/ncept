# Review escalation

The spaced-review Playground runs standalone: the queue, grading, and
scheduling are its own machinery, with no in-session model involvement.
This Skill is the model-judgment fragment: what to do when review evidence
can no longer move the estimate.

Reads: `pins()`, `trail(node)`, `competency(scope)`.

1. `(query)` `pins()` flags ceiling-pinned nodes: repeatedly easy at
   recognition or recall level while the estimate cannot rise further under
   the Pedagogical theory's rung ceilings.

2. `(model)` Escalate per node, and say why to the learner:
   - Swap the card format up the ladder (recall to application) when the
     node's criterion is still exercisable inside a card.
   - Hand the node to the lesson Skill for a transfer task when the next
     rung needs used or generalized evidence, which reviews alone never
     demonstrate.

3. Card proposals follow the standard contract: fronts exercise the
   criterion, misconception cards attack a recorded misconception (the
   misconception as distractor, its correction as answer), and the learner
   accepts, edits, or declines each. Nothing is created silently.
