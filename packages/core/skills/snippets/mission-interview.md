# Mission interview

`(model)` Ask why the learner wants this until the answer is a concrete
outcome ("read genomics papers by June" rather than "learn biology"), plus
explicit out-of-scope exclusions. On first contact the same conversation
gathers learning preferences. `(learner)` Confirms the statement and sets
the Mission's priority relative to their other Missions. Emit the Mission
Observation and any preference Observations.

Done when the Mission names an outcome, a rough horizon, and at least one
exclusion. Re-fires as a check-in when the target cone is exhausted or the
learner repeatedly overrides planning proposals.
