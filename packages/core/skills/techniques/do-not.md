# Techniques: do not

- Do not diagnose or match learning styles. No methodologically adequate
  evidence (Pashler et al. 2008).
- Do not use unassisted discovery for novices. It loses to explicit
  instruction (Alfieri et al. 2011, d ≈ −0.38 unassisted); guided discovery
  with feedback is a different, acceptable thing.
- Do not prescribe rereading or highlighting as practice. Low utility
  (Dunlosky et al. 2013); they build fluency illusions.
- Do not decorate materials. Seductive details reliably hurt novice learning
  (Mayer).
- Do not cite 2-sigma, 0.4 to 0.7 formative gains, or practice-makes-expert
  as magnitudes. The replicated numbers are 0.79, roughly 0.25, and 4% of
  variance in education respectively.
