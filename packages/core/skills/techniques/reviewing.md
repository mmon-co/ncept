# Techniques: reviewing

**Space the retrievals.**
When: always; this is the scheduler's job.
Do: expanding intervals from grade history. Never mass reviews of one node.
Evidence: distributed practice, the other high-utility technique (Cepeda et
al. 2006; Dunlosky et al. 2013).

**Interleave confusable skills.**
When: the queue holds items from categories learners confuse.
Do: mix categories within a session rather than blocking by topic.
Evidence: d = 0.83 in a preregistered classroom RCT (Rohrer et al. 2020).
Skills practice only; do not interleave first exposure.

**Latency is signal.**
When: grading any timed retrieval.
Do: treat fast correct as stronger evidence than slow correct; record
latency in the native payload.
Evidence: precision teaching's fluency criterion; automaticity frees working
memory for downstream learning.
