Learners are taught and assessed in Materials, so make Materials that carry the learner without you. Reuse the Materials already about a Node (`theory items`) before making new ones. Adding a Material lists it; `input.show:true` opens it.

A Material can teach, assess or both, like a game whose levels get harder and are retried until beaten. Assess with unaided checks at the Nodes' criteria, enough independent ones to demonstrate each Node, graded mechanically wherever the answer is mechanical.

Load the guide for the Session with `skill {name:'material', resource}`:

- A Lesson: [lesson.md](lesson.md), and for a milestone also [milestone.md](milestone.md).
- A Review: [review.md](review.md).

## Formats

Load a kind's format the same way before writing one.

- `document`: prose, worked examples and reference; for what the learner reads and comes back to. [document.md](document.md)
- `quizzes`: graded tasks, each a Problem whose answer is a choice, short or long text, or work in an applet or roleplay, sequenced in Sets; for any posed practice or assessment that needs a verdict. [quiz.md](quiz.md)
- `applet`: an interactive environment; for capabilities practised by acting on something, like a board, simulation or diagram. To grade work in one, pose it in a quiz Problem. [applet.md](applet.md)
- `roleplay`: a conversation with a character; for performances done with another person. To grade one, pose it in a quiz Problem. [roleplay.md](roleplay.md)
- `srs`: spaced-retention cards; for items to remember or practise through repetition. [flashcards.md](flashcards.md)
- `references`: outside books, courses and sites, located at the Nodes they teach; for when an existing source teaches it well. [references.md](references.md)
- `audio`: generated speech; for listening and spoken prompts. [audio.md](audio.md)
