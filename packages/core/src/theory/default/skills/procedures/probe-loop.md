Read `coverage(scope?)`, `probes(scope?)`, `items(node)` and `trail(node)`. Coverage finds untouched Nodes; probes lists suspect and probably-known Nodes with dates and what covers them. Neither chooses or stops the loop for you.

1. Agree a question/time budget and the decision it serves. Ask what the learner believes they know; record their words with `record_claim`, not preferences.
2. For calibration on a Mission's map, prepare one self-contained Problem per Node within the agreed budget, following `quiz-authoring.md`, and open one Problem Set with `"next":{"kind":"prerequisites"}`. For a focused follow-up, choose a failable task by capability, relevance and cost. State the unaided conditions before posing it.
3. Let the calibration Set choose its next Problem after each assessment: success implies prerequisites, failure implies dependents are not yet known. It stops when its covered Nodes are determined or no unasked Problem can change that. These are selection inferences, not evidence Records for unasked Nodes. The Playground records assessed outcomes; evaluate conversational free work through the Record tools. Inspect surprises and check the graph or pose a second check if it would change the next action.
4. Stop when the decision has enough evidence, the budget is spent, or the learner ends it. There is no hidden stopping threshold.
