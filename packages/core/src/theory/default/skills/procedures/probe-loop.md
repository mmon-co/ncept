Read `coverage(scope?)`, `probes(scope?)`, `items(node)` and `trail(node)`. Coverage finds untouched Nodes; probes lists suspect and probably-known Nodes with dates and what covers them. Neither chooses or stops the loop for you.

1. Agree a question/time budget and the decision it serves. Ask what the learner believes they know; record their words with `record_claim`, not preferences.
2. Choose a failable task by capability, relevance and cost. Use an authored item or make one lazily. State the unaided conditions before posing it.
3. Pose the task through the Quiz Playground's **open** or in conversation. The Playground records its own outcomes; evaluate free work through the Record tools. Inspect surprises and pose a second check if it would change the next action.
4. Stop when the decision has enough evidence, the budget is spent, or the learner ends it. There is no hidden stopping threshold.
