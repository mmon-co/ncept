Materials carry the learner into the work: teaching and assessment, or a brief and preparation for a real step. Reuse the Materials already about a Node (`theory items`) before making new ones.

A Material can teach, assess or both, like a game whose levels get harder and are retried until beaten. Assess with unaided checks at the Nodes' criteria under the Mission's assessment guidance, enough independent checks to demonstrate each Node, graded mechanically wherever the answer is mechanical. When the real work is the check, use its outcome rather than a separate practice set.

Load the guide for the Session with `skill {name:'material', resource}`:

- A Lesson: [lesson.md](lesson.md), and for a milestone also [milestone.md](milestone.md).
- A Review: [review.md](review.md).

## Which Material

- **Document:** prose, worked examples and reference, for what the learner reads and comes back to.
- **Quiz:** graded tasks, each answered by a choice, short or long text, or work in an applet or roleplay; for any posed practice or assessment that needs a verdict.
- **Applet:** an interactive environment, for capabilities practised by acting on something like a board, simulation or diagram. To grade work in one, pose it in a quiz task.
- **Roleplay:** a conversation with a character, for performances done with another person. To grade one, pose it in a quiz task.
- **Cards:** spaced-retention items, for what to remember or practise through repetition.
- **Reference:** an outside book, course or site located at the Nodes it teaches, for when an existing source teaches well.
- **Audio:** generated speech, for listening and spoken prompts.

## Assessing

- A choice shows recognition, so production is checked by producing. Competing options are alike in length and form, and each wrong one can reveal the error it stands for.
- Pose separate questions as separate short tasks. Give feedback on each answer in practice; hold results to the end for prior-capability, retention and milestone checks. Reserve one integrated response (an explanation, essay, project) for showing several capabilities at once, and describe what a response visibly does so a grader can tell shown from absent from mistaken.
- Help sets how far a task teaches. In a Lesson, show a hint after a mistake, or add the correction if the mistake persists; prior-capability, retention and milestone checks only measure. Help costs no evidence: one response counts once per Node however many checks touch it, and where help touches a check, that Node is recorded unaided as its checks stood before help and aided as they ended.
